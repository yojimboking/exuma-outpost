# Bespoke Services — Exuma Outpost

## Overview

The Exuma Outpost operates on a bespoke basis. While equipped with all modern amenities for your convenience, our management team assumes concierge duties and for a reasonable surcharge will equip you with everything you desire to make your trip as pleasant and memorable as it can be.

Use this menu for inspiration. Share your selections with Sharell and we'll do our best to accommodate.

> **Note:** Little Exuma is a remote island. Items requiring customs clearance may need 4-6 weeks advance notice. We employ a network of consolidators and expeditors, shipping cargo by sea or plane.

## Film & Photo Production

The Exuma Outpost is not only a luxury rental but also a **production paradise**. Conceived and managed by director/producer Marcus Nispel and Dyan Nispel, this is a reliable production setup in the Caribbean.

### Why Shoot Here
- Accommodates up to 12 guests in 6 rooms across three structures
- Large indoor and outdoor spaces
- Private cove at the beginning of Tropic of Cancer Beach
- White powder sand and crystal-clear water
- Best visibility for underwater photography in the world
- Picture-perfect interiors and exteriors

### Production Services
- Personal photographer
- Videographer
- Drone operator
- Hair & makeup artist (production experience)
